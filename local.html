🔒 Why 100% Local Processing?

Your private keys and certificates never leave your browser. Here's how it works.

WebAssembly Sandbox

OpenSSL is compiled to WebAssembly and runs in a sandboxed memory space inside your browser. It has zero network access — no data can leak, even in theory.

No Server Uploads

Unlike other SSL tools, your PFX files and private keys are never uploaded to a server. Every byte stays on your machine, processed entirely in the browser tab.

Open Source

The entire source code is available for inspection. You can verify that no telemetry or tracking is present. The OpenSSL WASM module is built from unmodified OpenSSL source.

Works Offline

Once the page loads, all PKI tools work without an internet connection. You can even disconnect your Wi-Fi and keep using the Certificate Decoder, PFX Extractor, and more.
